# assess_biological_control
Analysis of the reproducibility of a PCR internal control

**Tableau Dashboard:**
https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/jennifer.detweiler/viz/assess_bio_control_Tableau/Dashboard2?publish=yes

**Summary:**
I developed an internal assay control for the inagural PCR test at my place of work. This control worked well - to the point of also alarming my R&D group with an issue with our consumable lots. As an improvement to developing this control, we began more detailed monitoring of the control in terms of its metrics and instrument usage. The analysis posted here further summarizes the issues we saw within the manufacturing of consumables at our company. I submitted this project towards the completion of my master's degree, hence it's named Extra Credit.
